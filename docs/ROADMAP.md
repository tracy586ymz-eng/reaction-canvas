# Roadmap

These are planned directions, not shipped capabilities or promised dates.

- **Easy to try:** reproducible builds, Windows installation/capture validation, Mac customer-machine testing and transparent signing status.
- **Easy to contribute:** English UI, accessibility, synthetic provider compatibility fixtures and clear error states.
- **Better science:** image/structure confirmation before uncertain predictions, positive and negative feasibility evaluation cases across chemistry domains, provenance and uncertainty measurements.
- **Specialist components:** evaluate independent OCSR and chemistry models with separate weight/license checks; retain a general input workflow.
- **Better diagrams:** editable structure/conditions, complete balance validation where applicable, source annotations and literature evidence.

Start with a small issue and a reproducible example; do not upload private research, copyrighted article collections or model weights without the relevant rights.
